FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

What IT services does LD Technologies SRL offer?

Complete IT management (hardware, software and network functions), network infrastructure design, cybersecurity, virtualisation, custom software, websites and e-commerce, cloud computing, plus automation and collaborative robotics.

Why adopt a collaborative robot?

Cobots are designed to work safely in close contact with people, reducing the risk of injury or damage. They can work autonomously or alongside workers, increasing productivity and reducing downtime, and they adapt quickly to the job with simple tool changes and reprogramming. According to a study reported by the Financial Times, collaboration between robots and people is 85% more productive.

Which applications and industries does collaborative robotics cover?

Finishing, machine tending, quality control, assembly, palletising and welding. Industries served include food, industrial automation, metalworking, furniture, plastics and polymers, and technology.

How does a custom software project work?

In three phases: analysis of needs and processes to improve, design of a flexible and scalable architecture, development under strict control with testing and release. The client is always updated on development progress and can request changes along the way.

Which technologies do you use for software?

Web applications with ASP.NET, PHP, HTML and JavaScript; MySQL, SQL Server and Firebird databases; desktop applications in C/C++, C#, VB and Java. The best technology is chosen at design stage based on the client's needs.

Which robot manufacturers do you work with?

We work with Universal Robots (UR10e), Dobot (M1 Pro), MiR (MiR 1350), Microsoft (HoloLens 2, Mixed Reality), JAKA Robotics, Robotiq and ABB.
